KSISU Judge Screenshots
========================

All screenshots in this folder are byte-for-byte copies of the user-provided screenshots.
No screenshot pixels/content were edited. Only filenames and folders were changed for organization.

PDF_USE contains the recommended screenshots for the 10-page submission.
SUPPORTING_GUI contains the earlier/supporting GUI screenshots.

PDF_SCREENSHOT_MAP.csv maps recommended screenshots to PDF pages.
SCREENSHOT_INTEGRITY_SHA256.csv records SHA-256 hashes of the supplied screenshot bytes.
