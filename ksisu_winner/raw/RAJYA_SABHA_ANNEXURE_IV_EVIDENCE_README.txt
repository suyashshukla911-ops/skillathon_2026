Rajya Sabha Q131 Annexure-IV evidence
======================================

Official source:
https://sansad.in/getFile/annex/258/AU131.pdf?source=pqars

Document:
Government of India, Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship,
Rajya Sabha Unstarred Question No. 131, answered 07.12.2022.
Annexure-IV, PDF page 6.

The authoritative source is the official Rajya Sabha PDF above.
Because the original PDF binary could not be fetched into the build environment,
this folder contains a project-generated transcription/image of the Annexure-IV table
for audit convenience:
  - rajya_sabha_annexure_iv_page6_evidence.pdf
  - rajya_sabha_annexure_iv_page6_evidence.png

The transcription reproduces the Government ITI, Private ITI and Total columns
used in the model. All 30 positive historical analytical geography totals used
by this model are checked against the official table in the build.

Harmonisation notes:
- Historical source uses Bellary (89) as one geography; the model uses the
  harmonised Ballari-Vijayanagara analytical geography with the same 89 total.
- Historical source contains a duplicate/zero Uttara Kannada row and a zero
  Vijayapura row; those source artifacts are not separate analytical geographies.
- The counts are historical 2022 allocation weights, not current district seat totals.
