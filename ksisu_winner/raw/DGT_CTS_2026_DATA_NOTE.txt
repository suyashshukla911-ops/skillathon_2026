DGT CTS-2026 real public supply evidence

Source: Directorate General of Training, Provisional Dynamic Seat Matrix for Academic Session 2026.
Official page: https://www.dgt.gov.in/en/node/4675
Official PDF: provided in raw/dgt_cts_2026_provisional_seat_matrix.pdf

The DGT letter states that the tentative seat matrix was generated from approved instructor mappings as of 08.07.2026 and that the matrix could change until 20.07.2026. The final matrix would be generated after the cut-off. Therefore this package calls the source PROVISIONAL, not final.
